# AI Daily Log: April 30, 2026

## Key Focus Areas
- **Home Networking**: Configured WAN/LAN router ports and optical fiber connections.
- **Web Design**: Resolved background cropping issues in HTML layouts.

## Logged Activities & Context
### Fiber Optic Internet Setup
- **Context**: Setting up home Wi-Fi routers.
- **Actions Taken**: Documented WAN (Wide Area Network) vs. LAN (Local Area Network) port roles.
- **Key Takeaways**: ISP optical cables cannot plug directly into standard RJ45 WAN ports. They must first connect to an ONT (Optical Network Terminal) modem, which feeds the router via an Ethernet cable.

### CSS Background Alignment
- **Context**: Fixing cropped banner images in HTML.
- **Actions Taken**: Applied background positioning adjustments to force visibility of top image sections.
- **Key Takeaways**: Setting `background-position: top;` or `background-size: cover;` preserves the top edge of a banner regardless of viewport resizing.
