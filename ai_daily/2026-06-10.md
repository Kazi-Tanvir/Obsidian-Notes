## Logged Activities & Context
### Vapi System Prompt Engineering
- **Context**: Creating a medical voice receptionist agent for a clinic in Bangladesh.
- **Actions Taken**: Built a highly structured system prompt. Configured time-formatting conversion instructions (e.g. converting 16:30 to conversational Bengali "বিকাল ৪টা ৩০ মিনিট").
- **Key Takeaways**: Conversational rules prevent text-to-speech engine errors. Defined clear callback tools (booking, rescheduling).

### Voice Model Benchmarks
- **Context**: Identifying best-in-class Bangla VoIP performance.
- **Actions Taken**: Evaluated STT, LLM, and TTS layers on Vapi.
- **Key Takeaways**: Deepgram/Whisper-large-v3 serves best for STT, Claude-3-Haiku/GPT-4o-mini for speed-optimal LLM, and Play.ht for natural TTS outputs.

### Telephony Layer Mechanics
- **Context**: Integrating internet voice endpoints with local cellular grids.
- **Actions Taken**: Drafted a research roadmap on PSTN connections, SIP endpoints, and CPaaS gateways.
- **Key Takeaways**: Identified local IPTSPs (Internet Protocol Telephone Service Providers) providing SIP trunks in Bangladesh.

### ShareX Configuration

- **Actions Taken**: Addressed system audio recording errors inside the ShareX FFmpeg module.
- **Key Takeaways**: Enabled internal Realtek sound card routing (dshow Stereo Mix) to capture clean audio along with screen capture.
