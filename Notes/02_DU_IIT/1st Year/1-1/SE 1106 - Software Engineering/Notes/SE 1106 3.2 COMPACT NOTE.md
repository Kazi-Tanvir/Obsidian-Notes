## 3.2.3 Software Team

### Team Resource Options (n people, k years)

| Option | Key Idea | Verdict |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Individuals → tasks, little combined work | ✗ |
| 2 | Informal teams, ad hoc leader | ✗ |
| **3** | **Formal teams with defined structure** | **✓ Most productive** |

### Mantei's 3 Team Structures

| | DD | CD | CC |
|--|----|----|-----|
| **Leader** | None (rotating) | Defined + secondary | Single leader |
| **Decisions** | Consensus | Group solves, leader partitions | Leader decides |
| **Communication** | Horizontal | Horizontal + Vertical | Vertical |
| **Best for** | Difficult problems, long teams, low modularity, high sociability | Simple–moderate problems | Simple problems, large projects |
| **Defects** | More | Fewer | Fewer |
| **Speed** | Slower | Faster | Fastest |

### 7 Factors for Team Structure (Mantei)

> Difficulty · Size · Team lifetime · Modularity · Quality/Reliability · Delivery rigidity · Sociability

### Constantine's 4 Paradigms

| Paradigm | ≈ | Best For |
|----------|---|----------|
| Closed | CC | Similar past work |
| Random | — | Innovation / breakthrough |
| Open | DD+CC mix | Complex problems |
| Synchronous | — | Naturally compartmentalized problems |

### Chief Programmer Team

- **Chief Programmer** + **2–5 technical staff** + **Backup Engineer**
- **Librarian** → controls software configuration, catalogs reusable components

### Jelled Teams (DeMarco & Lister)

- "Whole > sum of parts" → high productivity, high motivation
- Need: **Trust** · **Right skills** · **Exclude mavericks** if needed

### 5 Team Toxins (Jackman)

| Toxin | Antitoxin Keyword |
|-------|-------------------|
| Frenzied atmosphere | Provide info, stable goals, early bad news |
| High frustration | Give decision-making authority |
| Poor procedures | Match process to software; let team choose |
| Unclear roles | Define roles early; formal reviews |
| Repeated failure | Team-based feedback; shared accountability |

## 3.2.4 Coordination & Communication

### Why projects struggle: **Scale** · **Uncertainty** · **Interoperability**

### 5 Coordination Techniques (Kraul & Streeter)

| Technique | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| Formal impersonal | Docs, memos, schedules, change requests |
| Formal interpersonal | Reviews, inspections |
| Informal interpersonal | Group meetings, co-location |
| Electronic | Email, bulletin boards, video conferencing |
| **Interpersonal networking** | **Informal discussions — rated HIGHEST value** |

> **Key finding:** Requirements & design reviews > code inspections in perceived value
