# CHAPTER 11: THREE-DIMENSIONAL SPACE; VECTORS

> To describe fully the motion of a boat, one must specify its speed and direction of motion at each instant. Speed and direction together describe a "vector" quantity.

In this chapter we discuss rectangular coordinate systems in three dimensions, and study the analytic geometry of lines, planes, and quadric surfaces. We develop the algebra and geometry of vectors, including the dot product, cross product, and scalar triple product, with applications to physical phenomena such as force, work, and torque. Finally, we explore cylindrical and spherical coordinate systems and their applications in astronomy and navigation.

---

## 11.1 RECTANGULAR COORDINATES IN 3-SPACE; SPHERES; CYLINDRICAL SURFACES

### 3-SPACE RECTANGULAR COORDINATES
* **Coordinate Axes & Planes:** Mutually perpendicular $x$-, $y$-, and $z$-axes meeting at origin $O(0, 0, 0)$, forming a **right-handed coordinate system**.
* **Coordinate Planes:** $xy$-plane ($z=0$), $xz$-plane ($y=0$), $yz$-plane ($x=0$), dividing 3-space into eight **octants** (first octant: $x>0, y>0, z>0$).

### DISTANCE FORMULA & SPHERES

> **Distance in 3-Space:** Between $P_1(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and $P_2(x_2, y_2, z_2)$:
> $$d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2} \tag{2}$$

> **Standard Equation of a Sphere (Center $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$, Radius $r$):**
> $$(x - x_0)^2 + (y - y_0)^2 + (z - z_0)^2 = r^2 \tag{4}$$

* **General Equation:** $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + Gx + Hy + Iz + J = 0$ (represents a sphere if $G^2 + H^2 + I^2 - 4J > 0$, a single point if equal to 0, or no graph if $<0$).

---

### CYLINDRICAL SURFACES (EXTRUSION)

> **11.1.2 THEOREM**
> An equation that contains only two of the variables $x, y, z$ represents a **cylindrical surface** generated by translating the 2D curve parallel to the coordinate axis of the missing variable.
> * Example: $x^2 + z^2 = 1$ is a right circular cylinder along the $y$-axis.
> * Example: $z = \sin y$ is a sine wave surface along the $x$-axis.

## 11.2 VECTORS

### GEOMETRIC VECTORS & VECTOR ARITHMETIC
* **Vector:** Specified by length (magnitude/norm $\|\mathbf{v}\|$) and direction. Zero vector is $\mathbf{0}$.
* **Equivalent Vectors:** Same length and direction ($\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{w}$).
* **Vector Addition (Parallelogram / Tip-to-Tail Law - 11.2.1):** $\mathbf{v} + \mathbf{w} = \mathbf{w} + \mathbf{v}$.
* **Scalar Multiplication (11.2.2):** $\|k\mathbf{v}\| = |k|\|\mathbf{v}\|$.

---

### COMPONENT FORM & STANDARD BASIS VECTORS
* **In 2-Space:** $\mathbf{v} = \langle v_1, v_2 \rangle = v_1\mathbf{i} + v_2\mathbf{j}$
* **In 3-Space:** $\mathbf{v} = \langle v_1, v_2, v_3 \rangle = v_1\mathbf{i} + v_2\mathbf{j} + v_3\mathbf{k}$
  $$\mathbf{i} = \langle 1, 0, 0 \rangle, \quad \mathbf{j} = \langle 0, 1, 0 \rangle, \quad \mathbf{k} = \langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$$
* **Vector between two points (Theorem 11.2.5):**
  $$\vec{P_1P_2} = \langle x_2 - x_1, y_2 - y_1, z_2 - z_1 \rangle$$
* **Norm:** $\|\mathbf{v}\| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + v_3^2}$.
* **Unit Vector (Normalization):** $\mathbf{u} = \frac{\mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}$.
* **Vector in terms of Length and Angle in 2-Space:** $\mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{v}\|\langle \cos\theta, \sin\theta \rangle$.

## 11.3 DOT PRODUCT; PROJECTIONS

### DEFINITION & PROPERTIES

> **11.3.1 DEFINITION**
> * In 2-Space: $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2$
> * In 3-Space: $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + u_3 v_3$

* **Theorem 11.3.2:** Commutative ($\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}$), Distributive ($\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v}+\mathbf{w}) = \mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v} + \mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{w}$), $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{v}\|^2$.

---

### ANGLE BETWEEN VECTORS & ORTHOGONALITY

> **11.3.3 THEOREM**
> $$\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|\|\mathbf{v}\|} \implies \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{u}\|\|\mathbf{v}\|\cos\theta \tag{2–4}$$

* $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are **orthogonal** $\iff \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$.
* **Direction Cosines (Theorem 11.3.4):**
  $$\cos\alpha = \frac{v_1}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}, \quad \cos\beta = \frac{v_2}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}, \quad \cos\gamma = \frac{v_3}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}, \quad \cos^2\alpha + \cos^2\beta + \cos^2\gamma = 1$$

---

### ORTHOGONAL PROJECTIONS & WORK
* **Orthogonal Projection of $\mathbf{v}$ on $\mathbf{b}$:**
  $$\text{proj}_\mathbf{b} \mathbf{v} = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|^2}\mathbf{b} \tag{12}$$
* **Vector Component Orthogonal to $\mathbf{b}$:** $\mathbf{v} - \text{proj}_\mathbf{b}\mathbf{v}$.
* **Work:** $W = \mathbf{F} \cdot \vec{PQ} = \|\mathbf{F}\|\|\vec{PQ}\|\cos\theta$.

## 11.4 CROSS PRODUCT

### DEFINITION & COMPUTATION

> **11.4.2 DEFINITION**
> $$\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\ v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \end{vmatrix} = (u_2 v_3 - u_3 v_2)\mathbf{i} - (u_1 v_3 - u_3 v_1)\mathbf{j} + (u_1 v_2 - u_2 v_1)\mathbf{k} \tag{4–5}$$

* **Anticommutative:** $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = -(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{u})$.
* **Orthogonality Property (11.4.4):** $\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}) = 0$ and $\mathbf{v} \cdot (\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}) = 0$.
* **Geometric Magnitude (11.4.5):**
  $$\|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}\| = \|\mathbf{u}\|\|\mathbf{v}\|\sin\theta = \text{Area of parallelogram with sides } \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}$$
* $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0} \iff \mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are parallel.

---

### SCALAR TRIPLE PRODUCT & VOLUME OF PARALLELEPIPED

> **11.4.6 THEOREM**
> $$\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) = \begin{vmatrix} u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\ v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \\ w_1 & w_2 & w_3 \end{vmatrix} \tag{9}$$
> * **Volume of Parallelepiped:** $V = |\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w})|$
> * $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w}$ are coplanar $\iff \mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) = 0$.

* **Torque / Vector Moment:** $\vec{PQ} \times \mathbf{F}$, Scalar Moment = $\|\vec{PQ} \times \mathbf{F}\|$.

## 11.5 PARAMETRIC EQUATIONS OF LINES

### PARAMETRIC & VECTOR EQUATIONS OF A LINE (Theorem 11.5.1)
Line through $P_0(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ parallel to $\mathbf{v} = \langle a, b, c \rangle$:
* **Parametric Form:**
  $$x = x_0 + at, \quad y = y_0 + bt, \quad z = z_0 + ct \tag{2}$$
* **Vector Form:**
  $$\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + t\mathbf{v} \tag{9}$$
* **Symmetric Equations ($a, b, c \neq 0$):**
  $$\frac{x - x_0}{a} = \frac{y - y_0}{b} = \frac{z - z_0}{c}$$

* **Distance from Point $P$ to Line through $A$ and $B$:**
  $$d = \frac{\|\vec{AP} \times \vec{AB}\|}{\|\vec{AB}\|}$$

## 11.6 PLANES IN 3-SPACE

### EQUATIONS OF PLANES

> **11.6.1 THEOREM**
> * **Point-Normal Form:** $a(x - x_0) + b(y - y_0) + c(z - z_0) = 0 \iff \mathbf{n} \cdot (\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}_0) = 0 \tag{1–3}$
> * **General Form:** $ax + by + cz + d = 0$, where normal $\mathbf{n} = \langle a, b, c \rangle$.

---

### INTERSECTING PLANES & DISTANCE FORMULAS
* **Acute Angle between Planes (with normals $\mathbf{n}_1, \mathbf{n}_2$):**
  $$\cos\theta = \frac{|\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2|}{\|\mathbf{n}_1\|\|\mathbf{n}_2\|} \tag{9}$$
* **Distance from Point $P_0(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ to Plane $ax + by + cz + d = 0$ (Theorem 11.6.2):**
  $$D = \frac{|a x_0 + b y_0 + c z_0 + d|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}} \tag{10}$$
* **Distance between Parallel Planes $ax+by+cz+d_1=0$ and $ax+by+cz+d_2=0$:**
  $$D = \frac{|d_1 - d_2|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}}$$

## 11.7 QUADRIC SURFACES

### CLASSIFICATION OF QUADRIC SURFACES (Table 11.7.1)

| Surface | Standard Equation | Traces |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ellipsoid** | $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} + \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1$ | Ellipses in all coordinate planes |
| **Hyperboloid of One Sheet** | $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1$ | Horizontal ellipses, vertical hyperbolas |
| **Hyperboloid of Two Sheets** | $\frac{z^2}{c^2} - \frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | Horizontal ellipses ($|z| \ge c$), vertical hyperbolas |
| **Elliptic Cone** | $z^2 = \frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2}$ | Horizontal ellipses, vertical intersecting lines |
| **Elliptic Paraboloid** | $z = \frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2}$ | Horizontal ellipses ($z > 0$), vertical parabolas |
| **Hyperbolic Paraboloid** | $z = \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{x^2}{a^2}$ | Horizontal hyperbolas, vertical parabolas (saddle point at origin) |

* **Volume of Ellipsoid:** $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi abc$.

## 11.8 CYLINDRICAL AND SPHERICAL COORDINATES

### DEFINITIONS & CONVERSIONS (Table 11.8.1)

* **Cylindrical Coordinates $(r, \theta, z)$:**
  $$x = r\cos\theta, \quad y = r\sin\theta, \quad z = z \quad (r \ge 0, \; 0 \le \theta < 2\pi)$$
  $$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}, \quad \tan\theta = \frac{y}{x}, \quad z = z$$
* **Spherical Coordinates $(\rho, \theta, \phi)$:**
  $$x = \rho\sin\phi\cos\theta, \quad y = \rho\sin\phi\sin\theta, \quad z = \rho\cos\phi \quad (\rho \ge 0, \; 0 \le \theta < 2\pi, \; 0 \le \phi \le \pi)$$
  $$\rho = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}, \quad \tan\theta = \frac{y}{x}, \quad \cos\phi = \frac{z}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}}$$

### CONSTANT SURFACES
* **Cylindrical:** $r = r_0$ (cylinder), $\theta = \theta_0$ (half-plane), $z = z_0$ (horizontal plane).
* **Spherical:** $\rho = \rho_0$ (sphere), $\theta = \theta_0$ (half-plane), $\phi = \phi_0$ (cone or $xy$-plane if $\phi_0 = \pi/2$).
